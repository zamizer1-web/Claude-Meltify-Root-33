# Redesign ideas (on hold: the creator is thinking up better ones)

Nothing here is decided. Kept so no idea is lost.

## Why

The AI playtest (rounds 1-3, `playtest/journal-*.md`) found that Root 33 plays like Root with an Expedition 33 coat of
paint: the speech bubbles, names and goal tracker carry all of the Expedition 33 feel (Kit: "90/10 Root"). The creator
wants a true combination of the two games.

## Idea 1: each character as its own faction (Ren)

`playtest/ren-faction-ideas.md`: all 11 characters as unique factions built from their Expedition 33 mechanics
(Gustave's Overcharge and Flags, Maelle's Stances, Lune's Stains, Sciel's Foretell and Twilight, Monoco's Bestial Wheel,
Verso's Perfection, Esquie's traversal, the Paintress's Number and Gommage, the Curator's workshop, Clea's monsters, the
White-Haired Man's erasure). Ren's first four: Gustave, the Paintress, Sciel, Esquie. Ren's main risk: only 4 of the 11
hold ground with an army, so the board could empty and stop feeling like Root.

How hard each is in the real Root (my estimate): easy-ish Gustave, Maelle, Verso; medium Sciel, the Paintress, Lune,
Monoco; hard Esquie (Root has no closed paths); very hard the Curator, Clea, the White-Haired Man.

## Idea 2: the creator's three layers (factions, hirelings, landmarks)

The creator noticed that some characters feel like Root's hirelings and some like its landmarks:

| Layer | Who | Built on (already in Root digital) |
|---|---|---|
| Playable factions (the Expedition party) | Gustave, Maelle, Lune, Sciel, Monoco, Verso | Ren's faction designs |
| Hirelings | the Paintress, Clea, the White-Haired Man | Last Dynasty, Warm Sun Prophets, Corvid Spies |
| Landmarks | Esquie, the Curator | the Ferry (rides along the river), the Legendary Forge (the anvil) |

Notes on it:
- The hardest faction ideas become reskins-plus-twist of hirelings and landmarks Root already runs (screens and bots
  included), so they are far easier to build.
- Hirelings put armies on the board, which answers Ren's empty-board risk.
- Esquie's rocks could stay as the Ferry's upgrades: each rock any player brings home unlocks one of his travel powers
  for everyone (Florrie swim, Dorrie smash, Soarrie fly, Urrie dive) and rewards that player.
- Needs the Hirelings & Landmarks DLC (Steam 3874110); the Ferry also needs the Underworld expansion. The six factions
  would not.
- A possible first four: Gustave and Monoco (armies), Sciel (Foretell marks), Maelle (lone duelist); Verso later because
  his identity is a major twist.
